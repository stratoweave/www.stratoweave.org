## Combine use cases. Make them your own.

Every operator has different priorities, systems, and ways of working.
StratoWeave gives you the building blocks to develop use cases around those
needs and combine them into a broader orchestration system. Shared models,
layered transforms, and operational state provide a foundation for connecting
capabilities across domains.

Adapt each use case to your needs: choose your service models, encode your
network policies, add device adapters, and connect your OSS/BSS through modeled
APIs. Start with one use case, then extend it or add new ones as your
requirements grow.

Have you implemented a use case with StratoWeave that you are ready to share?
Join a [community meeting](@/community/index.md) and present your work!
