The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) is the technical governing body of the
StratoWeave project. Its meetings are open to the public. These meetings are
technical and focus on the design of the core StratoWeave platform. We
welcome StratoWeave users, operators, and developers to attend and learn more
about upcoming features, design decisions, and the overall direction of the project.

## Meetings

The TSC meets **weekly on Tuesdays, 09:00–10:00 Central European time
(CET / CEST)**. Check the registration page for the time in your location.

- [Join the TSC meeting](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meeting/93700682952?password=43f5e7a2-028a-4acb-aa10-93abf8e22e83).
- [Read the TSC meeting minutes](https://lf-networking.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/StratoWeave/pages/1006010393).
- [Meeting recordings](https://openprofile.dev/my-meetings) are available through the Past Meetings tab in your LF profile.

## Bring a topic to the TSC

Sign in to the [TSC meeting workspace](https://lf-networking.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/StratoWeave/pages/1005813812)
with your [Linux Foundation ID](https://openprofile.dev/), edit the page, and add your
topic under “Request a Topic at an upcoming TSC Meeting.” TSC members set the final agenda.

If you need help finding the right discussion or cannot add a topic, contact
[info@stratoweave.org](mailto:info@stratoweave.org).
