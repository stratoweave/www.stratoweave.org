StratoWeave brings operators, developers, vendors, and integrators together around
model-driven, declarative orchestration. You can contribute code, test a deployment,
improve the documentation, or bring a network automation problem to the community.
The project has a neutral home at the [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/)
and is an [LF Networking](https://lfnetworking.org) project.

## Get involved

1. **Introduce yourself** in [Zulip chat](https://linuxfoundation.zulipchat.com/join/5eang5h2vl7wqhqdxobzxwat/) and tell us what you are working on.
2. **Come to a [community meeting](#community-meetings)** to meet contributors and discuss your ideas.
3. **Explore [ways to contribute](#ways-to-contribute)** and find where you can help.

## Ways to contribute

As an operator you can bring deployment requirements and operational experience
for various **use cases**.
Vendors and integrators can help build reusable integrations. Documentation, bug reports,
and reproductions are valuable contributions alongside code.

Useful contributions include YANG models, transforms, device adapters, tests,
use case requirements, documentation, and feedback from real networks. New to
StratoWeave? Start with the [tutorials](@/tutorials/_index.md) and the instructions
in the repository you are interested in contributing to.

## Collaborate across projects

We welcome collaboration with other LF Networking projects, open-source communities,
and standards development organizations. Join a community call to introduce your
project and the problem you want to solve, or contact
[info@stratoweave.org](mailto:info@stratoweave.org) for an introduction.

## General inquiries
Please contact [info@stratoweave.org](mailto:info@stratoweave.org) for general inquiries including questions about the project, community involvement, analyst inquiries, and media requests.
For security issues, please contact [security@stratoweave.org](mailto:security@stratoweave.org), review the [security policy](https://github.com/stratoweave/stratoweave/blob/main/SECURITY.md) for details.
